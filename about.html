About Our School

Utkarmit Madhya Vidyalaya is a government middle school located at Lohrapokhri, Satbarwa, Palamu, Jharkhand – 822126.

The school name may also be searched as Utkarmit Madhya Vidyalaya Lohrapokhri, Utkarmit Madhya Vidyalaya Lohra Pokhari, or Utkarmit Madhya Vidyalaya Lohra Pokhri. In Hindi: उत्क्रमित मध्य विद्यालय लोहरापोखरी.

This official information website provides school information, staff details, academic information, notices, photographs and location guidance for students, parents and the local community.
