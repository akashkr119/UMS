About Our School

Utkarmit Madhya Vidyalaya is a government middle school located in Lohrapokhri, Satbarwa, Palamu, Jharkhand. Our purpose is to provide inclusive, accessible and quality education and help children become responsible and confident citizens.

🛡️ Safe & Friendly Environment👨‍🏫 Learning Support📙 Activity Based Learning

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission
