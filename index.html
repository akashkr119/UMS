About Our School

Utkarmit Madhya Vidyalaya is a government middle school in Lohrapokhri, Satbarwa, Palamu, Jharkhand. The school may also be searched as Lohra Pokhari or Lohra Pokhri. In Hindi: उत्क्रमित मध्य विद्यालय लोहरापोखरी.
