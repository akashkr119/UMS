About us

A school rooted in its community

Education becomes meaningful when school, students, families and the community grow together.

Utkarmit Madhya Vidyalaya is located in Lohrapokhri, Satbarwa, Palamu district, Jharkhand. As a government middle school, our purpose is to support children with a strong educational foundation and an environment that encourages curiosity, confidence and responsible behaviour.

We value regular learning, respect, cleanliness, participation, teamwork and equal opportunity. This website is intended to make school information easier for students, parents and the local community to access.

Our Mission

To create a safe, inclusive and encouraging learning environment where every child can develop knowledge, skills, values and confidence.

Our Vision

Educated, responsible and capable young citizens contributing positively to their community and country.
