About the school

Learning with purpose, values, and opportunity

Utkramit Middle School is a community-centered institution that believes every child deserves a strong educational foundation. We nurture curiosity, discipline, and confidence through a balanced approach to learning.

Our mission is to prepare students not only for academic success but also for responsible citizenship and lifelong learning.

Our values

  • Excellence in education
  • Respect and discipline
  • Inclusion and care
  • Community partnership
