About the school

Learning with purpose, values, and opportunity

Utkarmit Madhya Vidyalaya is located in the Lohrapokhri area of Satbarwa, Palamu district, Jharkhand. The school serves the local community and works to provide children with a strong educational foundation.

Our aim is to encourage learning, discipline, confidence, responsible citizenship, and the overall development of every student.

Our values

  • Learning and educational growth
  • Respect and discipline
  • Inclusion and care
  • Community participation
